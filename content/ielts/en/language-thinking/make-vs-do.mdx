A **collocation** is a pairing that native speakers expect. *Make a decision* and *do research* are both correct, while *do a decision* is understood but sounds wrong.

## A useful tendency, not a rule

- **make** often goes with things that are *produced* or *created*: a plan, a decision, a mistake, progress.
- **do** often goes with *activities* or *tasks*: research, homework, damage, a favour.

Treat this as a tendency to notice, not a rule to memorise. When unsure, check a corpus or a learner's dictionary rather than guessing.

## In IELTS Writing

Accurate collocations are part of **Lexical Resource**. A less common word used in a natural collocation usually beats a rare word used awkwardly.
