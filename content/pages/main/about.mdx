<Chapter id="education" summary="IT at KMA, a B+ average, and a lot of respect for anyone with a 4.0." eyebrow="KMA" title="Education" album="school" image="/images/about/education.jpg" imageAlt="Classmates standing on a bridge railing on a grey day">

I study IT at the Academy of Cryptography Techniques (KMA). Right now that mostly means getting along with Linux — some days the terminal and I understand each other, some days we don’t.

My grades are decent: a GPA around 3.5, roughly a B+. I’m genuinely curious about the people who get a perfect 4.0 — how they study, how they think, how they sleep.

People like to argue about which field is easier, engineering or economics. After trying to keep up, my answer is simple: neither. Every field is hard once you take it seriously.
