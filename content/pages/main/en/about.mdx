I’m quite curious about the world around me. I like discovering new things and figuring out how they work.

I love conversations, swapping ideas and hearing different points of view. Sometimes that means a serious discussion about something fascinating; sometimes it’s just small talk, a few jokes, or a conversation with no topic at all.

I’m fairly introverted, even if it doesn’t show when we meet. I enjoy being around people, but I also really value time to myself. Some days I just want a quiet corner, an ordinary day, and a chance to let my mind rest.

<Chapter id="education" summary="IT at KMA, a B+ average, and a lot of respect for anyone with a 4.0." eyebrow="KMA" title="Education" album="school" image="/images/about/education.jpg" imageAlt="Classmates standing on a bridge railing on a grey day">

I study IT at the Academy of Cryptography Techniques (KMA). Right now that mostly means getting along with Linux — some days the terminal and I understand each other, some days we don’t.

My grades are decent: a GPA around 3.5, roughly a B+. I’m genuinely curious about the people who get a perfect 4.0 — how they study, how they think, how they sleep.

People like to argue about which field is easier, engineering or economics. After trying to keep up, my answer is simple: neither. Every field is hard once you take it seriously.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="leisure" summary="TFT, Arena of Valor, badminton, running, Rubik’s cubes, coffee and spontaneous trips." eyebrow="Beyond what I do" title="The things that make me, me" album="gaming" image="/images/about/leisure.jpg" imageAlt="[Describe the photo]">

I don’t want to introduce myself only through what I study or the work I’m chasing. There’s a lot more that makes me who I am.

I play **TFT (Teamfight Tactics)** and **Arena of Valor (Liên Quân)** — the photo sets from those games live in the gallery. I play **badminton** (not well, but I snap strings very consistently) and I go **running** to give my head a break.

I’ve been solving **Rubik’s cubes** for quite a while. I love finding the solution to a cube that looks like pure chaos — and sometimes I play simply because it’s fun.

I love nature, beautiful views and trips to places I’ve never been. I also like music, films, technology and plenty more. My interests are all over the place and often have nothing to do with each other, but I think that’s part of the fun.

I love coffee dates, catching up with friends, spontaneous trips and very ordinary moments. Sometimes a good meal — say, a bowl of avocado and mango with condensed milk — or a funny story is enough to make a day worth remembering.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="outlook" summary="Wanting to grow and do meaningful things — without forgetting to live now." eyebrow="How I look at life" title="Life, my way" image="/images/about/outlook.jpg" imageAlt="[Describe the photo]">

I have my own goals and wishes. I want to grow up, do things that matter, and build a future I can be proud of.

But I don’t think life is only about achievements, work, or getting better every single day.

I also want time for family, friends, my own hobbies and new experiences. I want to travel, see the world, meet interesting people and collect plenty of stories to tell.

I don’t want to be so busy building a good future that I forget to enjoy the life I have right now.

</Chapter>

<Chapter id="now" summary="21, still learning, experimenting and discovering who I am." eyebrow="Still exploring" title="Now & next" image="/images/about/now.jpg" imageAlt="[Describe the photo]">

I don’t have an answer for everything, and I don’t know exactly who I’ll be in a few years.

I’m still learning, experimenting, changing my mind, sometimes making mistakes, and discovering new sides of myself. Maybe I like that feeling — getting to experience things, to change, and to keep widening my world.

I made this website as a small corner of the internet to keep the things I care about, what I’m working on, the experiences I’ve had — and the thoughts I sometimes don’t know who to share with.

If you’ve made it here, I hope you know me a little better now — not only through what I do, but through how I live and see the world.

**I’m really glad we got to meet here!**

</Chapter>
