## A heading

Journal notes are plain Markdown/MDX. Headings build the "On this page" list, and every component from the IELTS articles works here too — `<Callout>`, `<Photo>`, `<Placeholder>`.

<Callout title="Tip">Keep notes short and frequent. A paragraph a week compounds.</Callout>
